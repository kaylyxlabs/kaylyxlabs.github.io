Version 1.0

Released 28 October 2026 · the first Google Play release · 1 min read

Your phone's health, every day

  • Today: a phone-health score with your companion, a check-up streak, and live battery, connection, screen-time and alarm cards.
  • 30-second daily check-up: battery, temperature, storage, memory, internet speed and sensors, scored out of 100, with quick wins.
  • Play: level up your companion with XP from check-ups and daily challenges, and collect badges.
  • Your week: health trend, screen time, internet speed, battery heat and steps, with a report card to share.

40+ tools, grouped by what you need

  • Sensors, level and angle finder, compass with true north, clock tools, device tests, network and speed tests, privacy checks, well-being timers and everyday utilities.
  • The timer and stopwatch stay in your notification tray.

And

  • Six companions, a companion stage on every tool, and a floating menu.
  • Home-screen widgets, from hourly to live.
  • A morning check-in and an evening summary at times you choose.
  • 15 languages, chosen in Settings.
  • Free with a small ad, with every other tool to try once a day. DeviceScope Pro is one payment that unlocks everything, removes ads and includes future updates.
