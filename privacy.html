DeviceScope privacy policy

Last updated: 28 October 2026 · 4 min read

DeviceScope ("the app") is made by Kaylyx Labs. This policy explains what the app does with information on your phone.

The short version

  • DeviceScope has no account and no analytics.
  • Everything the app measures stays on your phone. We do not collect, store on our servers, sell or share your measurements.
  • The free version shows ads from Google AdMob, which uses your phone's advertising ID (see "Ads" below). DeviceScope Pro has no ads.
  • Pro is bought through Google Play. We never see your payment details.
  • A few tools contact the internet only when you start them (see "Internet use" below).

What the app reads, and why

The app asks for each permission only when you open the tool that needs it. You can say no and keep using the rest of the app.

Settings, alarms, pinned tools, daily task results and the last sound reading are saved in the app's private storage on your phone. Uninstalling the app deletes them.

Internet use

  • Speed test and daily check-up: when you tap Start in the speed test, or run the daily check-up (which measures download speed for about five seconds), the app downloads and uploads test data with Cloudflare's public speed test service (speed.cloudflare.com). Like any website, Cloudflare sees your IP address. See Cloudflare's privacy policy: https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/
  • Ping, DNS lookup and stability monitor: when you run them, the app connects to the website you type, or to a public server (1.1.1.1), to time the connection.

  • Network info shows details about your own connection that Android provides; it does not look up your public IP address.

  • Update check: up to twice a day the app downloads a small file (latest.json) from our website, kaylyxlabs.github.io, to see whether a newer version is on Google Play. Nothing is sent except the normal request; GitHub, which hosts the site, sees your IP address.

Apart from ads, Google Play purchases (below) and the update check, no other part of the app uses the internet.

Ads

The free version shows a banner at the bottom of some tools, served by Google AdMob. To show ads, measure them and fight fraud, Google collects your device's advertising ID, your IP address and basic information about your phone and the ad, and may use them to show personalised ads where you allow it. In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, the app asks for your consent first; you can change your choice any time in Settings › Ad privacy choices. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings.

Ads are limited to content suitable for general audiences. How Google uses this data: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

DeviceScope Pro turns ads off completely: the ad service is never started.

Purchases

DeviceScope Pro is a one-time in-app purchase handled by Google Play. Google processes the payment under its own terms and privacy policy; the app only learns whether this Google account owns Pro, so it can unlock it on this and your other phones. We never receive your card, bank or billing details.

Children

DeviceScope is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children, and the app itself collects no personal information from anyone.
